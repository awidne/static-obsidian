# Gastrointestinal Tract
## Background
## Anatomy
### Organs

The retroperitoneal organs are summarized by the mnemonic SADPUCKER:
- Suprarenal glands
- Aorta and IVC
- Duodenum
- Pancreas
- Ureters
- Colon (ascending and descending)
- Kidneys
- Esophagus
- Rectum
### Mesentery
The ventral mesentery disappears after embryonic development except for:
- Falciform ligament (middle of anterior liver) - stabilizes the liver
- Lesser omentum (stomach and liver)
### Histology
The GI tract is made up of the:
- Mucosa
	- Epithelium
	- Lamina propria
	- Muscularis mucosae
- Submucosa
	- Mucularis externa (inner is circular, outer is longitudinal)
- Serous / adventitia
	- Visceral peritoneum (not for adventitia)
