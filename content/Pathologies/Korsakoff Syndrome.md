# Korsakoff Syndrome
## Background
### Definitions
Korsakoff syndrome (KS) is a late neuropsychiatric manifestation of [[Wernicke Encephalopathy|Wernicke encephalopathy]].

As with WE, this syndrome arises from thiamine deficiency.
### Etiology & Pathogenesis
80% of patients with severe alcohol use disorder recovering from WE exhibit findings of KS. It is a more common sequelae to alcohol-related WE than non-alcohol-related WE.

The manifestations of KS appears to be remnant neurologic findings following the resolution of acute WE manifestations.
## Clinical Presentation
### Signs & Symptoms
KS is characterized by:
- Marked deficits in anterograde and retrograde memory
- Apathy
- Intact sensorium
- Relative preservation of long-term memory/other cognitive skills

Confabulation may be present and insight is generally poor.
